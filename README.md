# Platform for Connecting Agricultural Cooperatives and Farmers

## Context and objectives of the project 

In Africa, the majority of farmers work in rural areas, sometimes without stable internet access and often with low literacy levels. Agricultural cooperatives play a key role in organizing, pooling resources, and marketing products. The government wants to establish a digital system for monitoring, controlling, and facilitating trade.
 
Goals : 
	• Create a centralized digital platform enabling: 
		o Connecting farmers and agricultural cooperatives . 
		o Monitoring and control of agricultural activities by the government. 
		o Farmers' access to information even without an Internet connection . 
	• simple and accessible interfaces (icons, audio, local multilingual). 
	• Ensure a modern and scalable architecture based on microservices .
